info About the Cause

Our mission is to reclaim neglected urban walls and transform them into beacons of community pride. This specific project aims to paint a sprawling mural depicting local history and future aspirations, using luminescent paints that glow in the deep navy-black atmospheric night.

Volunteers will be guided by master muralists. No prior experience is necessary—just a willingness to learn and contribute to a vibrant, large-scale piece of art that will stand as a testament to collective effort.
